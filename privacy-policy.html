Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2024

Horizon Education Institute ("Horizon", "we", "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of our students, applicants, staff, and website visitors. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we keep it safe.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, including:

  • Personal details such as your name, date of birth, and gender
  • Contact information such as email address, phone number, and postal address
  • Academic records, transcripts, and application documents
  • Payment and fee-related information where applicable
  • Information you provide when contacting us or using the student portal

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Process applications, admissions, and enrolment
  • Deliver teaching, assessments, and student support services
  • Communicate important academic and administrative updates
  • Maintain academic records as required by law and regulation
  • Improve our website, programmes, and services

3. Information We Share

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only where necessary with:

  • Regulatory and accreditation bodies as required by law
  • Service providers who support our operations, under strict confidentiality
  • Partner institutions for academic progression or certification
  • Third parties when you have given consent or when required by law

4. Data Security

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Access to personal data is restricted to staff who need it to perform their duties.

5. Cookies & Website Analytics

Our website may use cookies to improve your browsing experience and to understand how visitors use the site. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not work as intended.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Request deletion of your information, subject to legal obligations
  • Object to or restrict certain processing activities

7. Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law — for example, maintaining academic records for verification by employers and institutions.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page with an updated revision date.

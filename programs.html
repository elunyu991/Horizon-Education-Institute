Entry Requirements by Level

Clear, fair admission criteria for every level of study. International students have their qualifications equated by UNEB.

🎓 Diploma Programs

  • Uganda Advanced Certificate of Education (UACE) with at least 1 Principal Pass and 2 Subsidiaries in relevant subjects; OR
  • A relevant Certificate from a recognised institution; OR
  • A Higher Education Access Certificate (HEAC).

📜 Certificate Programs

  • Uganda Certificate of Education (UCE) with at least 5 passes; OR
  • Relevant work experience for mature applicants (25+ years).

🧰 Short & Professional Courses

  • Open to all — no formal academic entry requirements
  • Basic literacy and numeracy recommended
  • Ideal for school leavers, professionals, and entrepreneurs

🌍 International Students

  • Academic documents obtained outside Uganda must be equated by UNEB before submission
  • Proof of English proficiency required
  • Dedicated international student support

Higher Education Access Certificate (HEAC)

Our one-year Higher Education Access Certificate bridging program prepares students who did not attain enough principal passes at A-Level — or whose international secondary qualifications need equivalence — for direct entry into diploma and degree programs. Tracks available in General Studies, Biological Sciences, and Physical Sciences.

Entry: 2 Subsidiaries and 1 Principal Pass at UACE, or equivalent

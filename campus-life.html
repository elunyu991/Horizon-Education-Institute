Campus Life at Horizon

A vibrant, inclusive, and empowering environment where students thrive academically, socially, and personally.

Student Life & Activities

Horizon Education Institute offers a dynamic campus experience that goes beyond the classroom. From student clubs and leadership programs to cultural events and sports, our campus is a hub of creativity, collaboration, and community.

Accommodation & Housing

Comfortable, secure, and affordable on-campus and affiliated housing for every student.

🏠

On-Campus Hostels

Separate male and female hostels with resident wardens, secure rooms, and communal study areas.

🛡️

24/7 Security

Gated campus with round-the-clock security, night patrols, and a safe environment for all residents.

🚿

Utilities & Care

Water, electricity, and basic amenities included in hostel fees, with laundry and catering options nearby.

🤝

Off-Campus Guidance

Our welfare office helps students find verified, affordable rentals close to campus.

Students' Guild

The Horizon Students' Guild is the democratically elected student government that champions student welfare, organises cultural activities, and represents student voices to the institute's leadership. Led by a Guild President and cabinet, it coordinates:

  • Orientation programmes for new students
  • Cultural galas, music festivals, and talent shows
  • Student welfare, feedback, and complaints resolution
  • Community outreach and volunteer initiatives
🎓

Guild Leadership

Run for office, vote for your representatives, and build leadership experience that employers value. Guild elections are held annually each semester.

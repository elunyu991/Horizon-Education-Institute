Admission Requirements

  • Diploma Programs: Uganda Certificate of Education (UCE) and Uganda Advanced Certificate of Education (UACE) or equivalent.
  • Certificate Programs: UCE or equivalent with relevant experience.
  • International Students: Must provide certified academic transcripts and proof of English proficiency.
  • Mature Entry: Applicants aged 25+ with relevant experience may apply through the mature entry scheme.

How to Apply

  1. Visit our online application portal or pick up a form from the admissions office.
  2. Fill in your personal and academic details accurately.
  3. Attach certified copies of academic documents and national ID/passport.
  4. Pay the application fee via bank or mobile money and attach proof of payment.
  5. Submit your application and await confirmation via email or SMS.

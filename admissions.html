Admission Requirements

🎓 Diploma Programs

  • Uganda Advanced Certificate of Education (UACE) with at least 1 Principal Pass and 2 Subsidiaries in relevant subjects; OR
  • A relevant Certificate from a recognised institution; OR
  • A Higher Education Access Certificate (HEAC).

📜 Certificate Programs

  • Uganda Certificate of Education (UCE) with at least 5 passes; OR
  • Relevant work experience for mature applicants.

🧰 Short & Professional Courses

  • Open to all — no formal academic entry requirements
  • Basic literacy and numeracy recommended

🌍 International Students

  • Qualifications obtained outside Uganda must be equated by UNEB before submission
  • Proof of English proficiency required
  • Mature entry scheme available for applicants aged 25+

How to Apply

Application Steps

  1. Complete the online application form or collect one from the Admissions Office.
  2. Fill in your personal and academic details accurately.
  3. Attach certified copies of academic documents and a national ID/passport.
  4. Pay the UGX 50,000 application fee via bank or mobile money and attach proof of payment.
  5. Submit and await confirmation via email or SMS.

Required Documents

  • Certified copies of UCE and UACE certificates/transcripts (or equivalent)
  • National ID or passport (2 passport photos)
  • Completed application form with proof of application fee payment
  • Recommendation letters (if requested)
  • Health certificate (during physical enrolment)

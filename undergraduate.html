Undergraduate Programs

Certificate and diploma qualifications that combine classroom theory with hands-on, practical training for the modern workplace.

Why Choose Our Undergraduate Programs?

At Horizon Education Institute, undergraduate study is built around applied learning. Our programmes are developed in consultation with employers and industry bodies, so the skills you gain are the skills the market needs — whether you are starting your career or advancing within your profession.

🎓

Nationally Recognised

Certificates and diplomas aligned to national qualifications frameworks and recognised by employers across East Africa.

🔧

Practical-First Training

Well-equipped labs, workshops, and studios give you real experience before you graduate.

🤝

Industry Attachments

Internship and industrial attachment placements connect you to employers from your first year.

📈

Clear Progression

Certificate graduates can progress into diploma and higher qualifications at Horizon and beyond.

Diploma Programs

Our diploma programmes provide advanced knowledge and skills over a two-year period, including supervised industrial training.

  • Diploma in Computer Science — programming, systems design, networking, and database management.
  • Diploma in Business Management — business administration, leadership, marketing, and entrepreneurship.
  • Diploma in Accounting — financial accounting, auditing, taxation, and payroll management.
  • Diploma in Journalism & Mass Media — advanced reporting, broadcasting, and media production.
  • Diploma in Fashion & Garment Design — fashion illustration, pattern cutting, and garment construction.

Entry Requirements

Certificate Programs

  • Uganda Certificate of Education (UCE) or equivalent
  • Pass in English and Mathematics is an added advantage
  • Mature-age entry available for applicants with relevant work experience

Diploma Programs

  • Uganda Advanced Certificate of Education (UACE) or equivalent
  • Certificate holders with at least a pass in the relevant field may upgrade
  • Work experience in a related area may be considered

How to Apply

  • Step 1: Complete the online application form or collect a form from the Admissions Office.
  • Step 2: Submit certified copies of your academic documents and a passport photo.
  • Step 3: Attend an interview or placement assessment if required.
  • Step 4: Receive your admission letter and report for orientation.

Questions? Visit our Admissions page or contact us — we are happy to help.

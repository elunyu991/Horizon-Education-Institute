Online Learning

Quality Horizon education, wherever you are. Flexible online and blended programmes for learners across Uganda and beyond.

Study Your Way

Horizon Education Institute brings its practical, industry-focused teaching online. Our learning platform hosts course materials, recorded and live classes, assignments, and assessments — so you can study from home, work, or anywhere with an internet connection, while still enjoying support from dedicated tutors.

Recognised Certificates

Complete your course and earn the same recognised certificates awarded to on-campus students.

How Online Learning Works

  • Enrol online — complete the application form and receive your login details.
  • Get oriented — attend a short virtual orientation on using the learning platform.
  • Learn — follow weekly modules, watch recorded lessons, and join live sessions.
  • Practise — complete assignments, quizzes, and practical projects with tutor feedback.
  • Assess & certify — sit assessments (online or at a nearby centre) and receive your certificate.

About Horizon Education Institute

A world-class centre for quality, affordable, community-tailored higher education in the heart of Soroti, Uganda.

Who We Are

Horizon Education Institute is a premier institution dedicated to delivering high-quality, practical education that meets global standards while remaining rooted in local relevance. Our programs are designed to equip students with the knowledge, skills, and values needed to thrive in today's dynamic world.

From technology and business to journalism, education, and vocational skills, we offer a diverse range of academic pathways tailored to meet the needs of modern learners and employers. Our faculty are experienced professionals committed to mentorship, innovation, and excellence.

We serve individuals and communities in Uganda and the wider East African region through partnership in higher education, training, and capacity building.

Our Journey

From a small training centre in 1999 to a vibrant multi-school institute serving thousands of students today.

1999
Founded in Soroti — Horizon began as a community skills centre offering secretarial and computer courses.
2005
First national certificates — our ICT and business programmes received national accreditation.
2012
Diploma programmes launched — computer science, business management, and journalism & mass media.
2018
Campus expansion — new workshops, labs, and a modern library block at Senior Quarters, Soroti City West.
2023
Professional & online learning — short courses, e-learning, and graduate diplomas added.
Today
Five schools, 40+ programs — serving 2,500+ students with a 95% graduate employment rate.

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To respond to the needs of individuals and communities in Uganda and the Great Lakes region through partnership in higher education, training, and capacity building — equipping learners with knowledge, skills, and values for sustainable development.

Our Vision

To be a leading centre of academic excellence and professional growth in East Africa — a world-class destination for quality, affordable, community-tailored higher education.

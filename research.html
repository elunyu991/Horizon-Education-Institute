Research & Innovation

Applied research that solves real problems — led by our staff and advanced students, in partnership with industry and the community.

Research with Impact

Research at Horizon Education Institute is practical by design. Our research agenda focuses on technology, vocational education, enterprise development, and community wellbeing — producing findings that inform teaching, support local business, and contribute to national development.

🔬

Applied Research Centres

Focused teams exploring ICT innovation, technical skills development, and agribusiness solutions.

📝

Student Research

Diploma and graduate students undertake guided research projects that tackle real community and industry challenges.

🏭

Industry Partnerships

Collaborative projects with employers and organisations that turn ideas into products, services, and policy.

📖

Publications & Events

Research papers, technical reports, and an annual research day where findings are shared publicly.

Research Focus Areas

Information & Communication Technology

Software solutions, digital literacy, and technology adoption for small businesses and schools.

Technical & Vocational Education

Improving how skills are taught, assessed, and aligned to labour market demand.

Enterprise & Agribusiness

Feasibility studies, value addition, and market research supporting local entrepreneurs and farmers.

Media & Communication Studies

Media practices, digital journalism, and communication for development.

Hospitality & Food Science

Food safety, quality, and hospitality service innovations for the tourism sector.

Community Development

Studies addressing local challenges in education, health, and economic opportunity.

  • Co-fund innovation that benefits your organisation

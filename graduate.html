Graduate Programs

Postgraduate diplomas and professional advancement pathways for graduates and working professionals seeking to lead.

Advance Beyond the Ordinary

Our graduate offerings are designed for diploma and degree holders who want to deepen their expertise, specialise in a field, or step into management and leadership roles. Programmes combine rigorous coursework with applied projects, mentoring, and industry networking.

Graduate Programmes

Postgraduate Diploma in Management

Intensive professional development workshops in leadership, finance, and digital skills for organisations.

Entry Requirements

Postgraduate Diplomas

  • Relevant diploma or bachelor's degree from a recognised institution
  • At least one year of relevant work or professional experience
  • Recent graduates may apply through the Academic Registrar

Executive & Professional Programmes

  • Open to professionals with relevant experience
  • Academic requirements may be waived based on experience
  • Group and corporate enrolments welcome

How to Apply

  • Step 1: Submit the online application with your academic transcripts.
  • Step 2: Provide your professional CV and two references.
  • Step 3: Attend an interview with the programme coordinator.
  • Step 4: Receive your admission letter and enrolment pack.

Contact the Admissions Office or call +256 774-390-641 for guidance.
